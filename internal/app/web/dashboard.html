Sharing session

Your old host/guest settings and files are preserved. All computers need this version and fresh pairing. Rename every .folderbridgeignore to .fbignore before starting.

Create a session, then invite your other computers. The creator has no special role.

Use a dedicated folder. The first sync merges existing files; concurrent contents are preserved. Rules in .fbignore and .gitignore apply to this computer only. Empty directories and links are not replicated.

Stopping or pausing preserves your session and files. Leaving keeps files and history, but requires fresh pairing to join a different session.

Session members

    Any reachable members can sync without the creator. Offline members keep their files; their pending changes reconcile when they return. All members must be trusted.

    Invite another computer

    On the other computer, choose Join session, select its local folder, and paste a reachable link below. Invitations are passwords: anyone with one can join and invite others.

    The inviting member must be online to complete joining, but need not stay online afterward. Every computer needs authorized inbound network access on TCP 48713. No automatic LAN discovery.

    Windows integration

    Starts minimized to the system tray for this user. No administrator rights required.

    Ignore rule inspector

    Inspect this computer's rules for a relative file path. Other members apply their own rules.
